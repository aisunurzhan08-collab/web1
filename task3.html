About Kairat Nurtas

Kairat Nurtas is a famous Kazakh singer and actor. He was born on February 25, 1989, in Turkestan, Kazakhstan. He started performing at a young age and became popular for his Kazakh songs.

Important Events

  1. He was born in 1989.
  2. He started performing as a child.
  3. He studied at a music college.
  4. He held his first solo concert in 2008.
  5. He continued his career in music and film.
